The Definition of Cross-border E-commerce

Cross-border e-commerce is a kind of e-commerce. As the largest e-commerce platform in the world, Amazon entered Shanghai Free Trade Zone on August 20, 2014. The memorandum signed on that day shows that "Amazon will open its new cross-border e-commerce platform in the free trade zone." So it is clear that cross-border e-commerce is a new form of international e-commerce.

Then, what exactly is the definition of cross-border e-commerce? Cross-border e-commerce refers to an international business activity in which trading entities from different customs areas conduct transactions, complete payment and settlement, and deliver goods through cross-border logistics, based on e-commerce platforms.

In terms of business models, cross-border e-commerce can be divided into cross-border retail e-commerce (B2C and C2C) and cross-border B2B e-commerce. In terms of customs areas, cross-border e-commerce can be divided into cross-border import e-commerce and cross-border export e-commerce. 1. Cross-border Retail E-Commerce (B2C, Business-to-Consumer) Cross-border B2C e-commerce refers to the international business activities of companies in different customs areas directly selling products or services to consumers through the e-commerce platform. The process includes reaching transactions, completing payment and delivery of goods through cross-border logistics. Sellers publish product information, price and other contents, and individual buyers can search the information and place orders directly.

2. Cross-border B2B E-Commerce (B2B, Business-to-Business)

Cross-border B2B e-commerce refers to the international business activities between companies in different customs areas. The process includes reaching the transaction, completing the payment and settlement through e-commerce platforms and delivering the products through cross-border logistics, and completing the transaction.

3. Cross-border Import and Export Business Process

From the process of import and export through cross-border e-commerce, after the products are recorded on the platform of cross-border e-commerce, producers or manufacturers will deliver the products to logistics for shipment. After customs clearance and commodity inspection in both the export and import countries, the products are finally delivered to the consumers.

With the continuous development of cross-border e-commerce, cross-border e-commerce involves the whole industry chain such as logistics, warehousing, payment, customs clearance, etc. The whole industry ecosystem is becoming more and more complete, and the division of labor is increasingly clear.
