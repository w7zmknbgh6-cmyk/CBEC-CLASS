三、写作框架（基础版 · 填空成文）

下不了笔？先把框架填满，再润色连接。

Welcome to our store! We are ________（定位：一家专营……的店铺）. Our business scope covers ________（主营品类）. All products are ________（优势：工厂直供/专利认证）, so our prices are ________ and the quality is ________. We offer ________（退货政策）, and the products will be delivered within ________（发货时间）. If you have any problems, please ________（售后动作）. Follow us to get ________（结尾：优惠券/新品信息）!

例文 1 · 商家店铺（digital products，约 105 词）
Welcome to TechStar! Established in 2015, we are a professional cross-border e-commerce store specializing in digital products. Our business scope covers phone cases, chargers, Bluetooth earphones and other smart accessories, and we release new products every season. All products are directly supplied by our own factory, so our prices are competitive and the quality is credible. We have passed ISO9001 certification and own 3 patent certificates. We offer a 30-day return policy and 24/7 after-sales service; the products will be delivered within 48 hours. Follow our social media accounts to get the latest news and coupons — Don't hesitate, start your shopping journey now!
欢迎来到 TechStar！我们成立于 2015 年，是一家专营数码产品的跨境电商店铺。业务范围涵盖手机壳、充电器、蓝牙耳机及智能配件，每季上新。所有产品工厂直供，价格有竞争力、品质可信。我们已通过 ISO9001 认证并拥有 3 项专利。提供 30 天退货政策与全天候售后服务，48 小时内发货。关注我们的社媒账号获取最新资讯和优惠券——别犹豫，现在就开始购物之旅吧！
亮点解析：
  • 四问齐全：定位（第 1—2 句）→ 业务范围（第 2 句）→ 优势实力（第 3—4 句）→ 物流售后（第 5 句），最后促销收尾。
  • 优势有证据：不只是 best quality，而是 ISO9001 认证 + 3 项专利 + 工厂直供。
  • 红色词全部来自上方提示词库——例文就是提示词库的“组装成品”。
例文 2 · 个人店铺（mom & baby，约 90 词）
Hi and welcome to Sunny Baby! We are a personal store focusing on mom & baby products. Our cute and warm store decoration will make you feel at home. Our main products are baby carriers, mummy bags and milk storage bags, all carefully selected from reliable suppliers. Every product is checked one by one before shipping. If you have any questions, just message us — we will reply within 12 hours. New buyers can get a registration coupon for the first order. Enjoy your shopping!
欢迎来到 Sunny Baby！我们是一家专注母婴产品的个人店铺。温馨可爱的店铺装修让您有宾至如归的感觉。主营婴儿背带、妈咪包和储奶袋，均从可靠供应商精心挑选。每件产品发货前逐一检查。有任何问题请随时私信，我们 12 小时内回复。新买家首单可领注册优惠券。祝您购物愉快！
亮点解析：
  • 个人店铺写法更简洁（呼应 Text A：personal store 描述 concise、注重装修），用装修与色彩替代公司资质。
  • 保障不减配：虽是个人店，仍写了检查流程 + 12 小时回复 + 首单优惠券。
  • 语气亲切（Hi / Enjoy your shopping!），与商家店的专业口吻形成对比——两种文风都值得模仿。

例文 · 保温杯 Smart Thermal Bottle（FAB 标注版，约 120 词）
Are you still worried that your coffee gets cold too fast? If so, this Smart Thermal Bottle is highly recommended to you.

Feature 特征】【Made of food-grade 304 stainless steel, it measures 26 cm and holds 500 ml. The leak-proof lid comes with a one-touch button.
Advantage 优势】【Unlike the normal bottle, it keeps drinks hot for up to 12 hours, and you'll never worry about spills in your bag.
Benefit 利益】【Perfect for office, travel and outdoor sports — it saves your time and gives you a warm drink anytime, anywhere.
Specifications: 500 ml / 26 cm / Silver, White, Pink.
So what are you waiting for? Don't hesitate, add it to your cart and enjoy free shipping now!
还在担心咖啡凉得太快吗？如果是，这款智能保温杯强烈推荐给您。食品级 304 不锈钢制成，高 26 厘米、容量 500 毫升；防漏杯盖带一键开关。与普通杯子不同，它能保温长达 12 小时，放包里绝不用担心洒漏。办公、旅行、户外运动都非常适合——为您节省时间，随时随地喝上一口热饮。规格：500 毫升 / 26 厘米 / 银、白、粉三色。还等什么？别犹豫，加入购物车，现在下单即享包邮！
亮点解析：
  • 结构即范文：Feature → Advantage → Benefit 三层清晰标注，写作时照此分层即可。
  • 开场是问句钩子（Are you still worried that...?），结尾是 CTA（What are you waiting for?）——首尾都抓人。
  • 例句与课文沙发案例同源：unlike the normal... / never worry about... / perfect for... / don't hesitate 全部可迁移。
