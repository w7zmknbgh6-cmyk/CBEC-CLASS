阅读理解 46—50 · 原文精讲

红色 = 生词，点击可显示词义

When people talk about shopping across borders, they usually think of cheap goods and fast delivery. In fact, the most important change of the past ten years is not the price but the way a small seller reaches a customer eight thousand kilometres away.

In the old model, a factory sold to an exporter, the exporter sold to a wholesaler, and the wholesaler sold to a retailer. Every step added a margin and took time. A platform cuts most of these steps: the seller lists the goods, the buyer pays into an escrow account, and the platform releases the money only after the parcel is confirmed as received.

Payment is the second pillar. Because the two sides use different currencies and often do not trust each other, third-party payment services hold the money in the middle. Settlement becomes a matter of a few days instead of a letter of credit that may take weeks to clear.

Logistics decides whether the promise can be kept. A parcel that crosses three borders may pass through several customs points, and one missing document can stop the whole journey. This is why experienced sellers keep popular items in overseas warehouses, even though storage there costs more than at home.

None of this makes cross-border selling easy. Rules on tax, product safety and personal data are becoming stricter in almost every market, and a seller who ignores them will lose the account, not just the order. The winners of the next few years will be those who treat compliance, logistics and service as one system rather than three separate costs.

写作 61 · 题目与范文

请以 “A Best-selling Cross-border E-commerce Product” 为题，用英语介绍一样跨境电商热卖产品，字数不少于 120 词。 提示要点：① 产品名称（name）与品类；② 外观与颜色（appearance and colour）；③ 主要功能或特点（functions / features）；④ 适用人群或使用场景（target users / scenes）；⑤ 热卖原因，如价格、物流或售后服务（why it sells well）。
One of the best-selling products on cross-border platforms this year is a portable mini blender. It is a small kitchen appliance, about the size of a water bottle, and it comes in white, pink and light blue. The body is made of food-grade plastic with a stainless-steel blade, so it is light and easy to carry. Its main function is to make juice, smoothies and baby food in less than thirty seconds. It has a rechargeable battery, which can be used about twenty times after one charge, and the cup can be washed directly under the tap. It is popular with office workers, students and young parents, because they can enjoy fresh drinks at work, in the dormitory or while travelling. The product sells well for three reasons. First, it is cheap: most buyers pay less than twenty dollars. Second, it is small and light, so the shipping cost is low and delivery is fast. Third, sellers offer a one-year guarantee and a quick refund if the product is damaged on the way. In short, a good cross-border product must be useful, easy to ship and well supported after the sale. (about 185 words)
